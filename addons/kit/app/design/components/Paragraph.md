# Paragraph

Wrapping text in the project reading face.

## When to use

Use it for explanatory content in an app, a document or a presentation. Keep long paragraphs in a width-limited Content region.

## What you provide

Supply text. Settings font_content and sizes.content provide the defaults; size, family, color and line_height can override one paragraph. selectable enables copying.

The generated [component reference](../../../bindings/reference.md) lists every prop and binding spelling.

## Do

- Reuse project fonts, colors and sizes through Settings.
- Check the composition at narrow widths and in both appearances.

## Don't

- Bake a specific presentation theme into application content.
- Rely on invisible content to keep background work running or stop it.
