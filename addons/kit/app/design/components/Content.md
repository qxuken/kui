# Content

A reading composition using project spacing and optional layout presets.

## When to use

Use ordinary flow for documents, editorial for a story with a supporting visual, briefing for a narrow introduction beside evidence, or split for a dominant aside. Minimal keeps one reading column.

## What you provide

Supply children and optionally aside, title, subtitle and eyebrow. Layout and theme are independent. A split aside uses the accent surface: choose content colors that read on it. grow and a title/section preset enable vertical centering.

The generated [component reference](../../../bindings/reference.md) lists every prop and binding spelling.

## Do

- Reuse project fonts, colors and sizes through Settings.
- Check the composition at narrow widths and in both appearances.

## Don't

- Bake a specific presentation theme into application content.
- Rely on invisible content to keep background work running or stop it.
